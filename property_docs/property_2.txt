PROPERTY LEASE AGREEMENT & WELCOME PACKET - MAPLEWOOD HEIGHTS

1. INTRODUCTION AND APARTMENT FEATURES
Maplewood Heights presents Unit 12A, a cozy and highly efficient 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom loft situated at 1044 Maple Street. This unit features striking exposed brick accent walls, high 12-foot ceilings, in-unit washer and dryer appliances, and an updated modern kitchen with quartz countertops and custom wooden cabinetry. It is specifically designed for working professionals seeking a low-maintenance urban lifestyle close to downtown business districts, public transit hubs, and popular dining locations.

2. RENTAL PAYMENTS, SECURITY DEPOSIT, AND FEES
The monthly rental rate for Unit 12A is $1,600, due on the first calendar day of every month. A grace period is extended until the 4th of the month, after which a 5% late fee applies automatically. The security deposit equals exactly one month's rent ($1,600) and is held in an interest-bearing escrow account during the duration of the lease term. Key replacement costs $50 per physical key or electronic fob if lost, and returned check fees are assessed at $35 per incident.

3. UTILITIES, SERVICES, AND PARKING ARRANGEMENTS
Gas and electricity are metered separately and paid directly by the tenant to the local utility providers before the monthly due dates. Water and community trash disposal services are fully covered by the landlord. One uncovered parking stall is included in the lease agreement at no extra charge. Additional guest parking passes can be requested from the main leasing office for a nominal monthly fee of $25, subject to availability.

4. COMMUNITY AMENITIES AND HOUSE POLICIES
Tenants enjoy 24-hour secure access to package lockers, a dedicated bicycle storage room, and the outdoor courtyard BBQ grill station. Subletting, short-term rentals, or listing the apartment on platforms like Airbnb are strictly forbidden without prior written consent from property management. All pets must be registered with the management office prior to move-in, with a strict maximum limit of two domestic pets per household.
