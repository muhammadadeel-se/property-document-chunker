PROPERTY LEASE AGREEMENT & WELCOME PACKET - THE OAKWOOD RESIDENCES

1. PROPERTY OVERVIEW AND APARTMENT SPECIFICATIONS
Welcome to The Oakwood Residences, located at 742 Evergreen Terrace. This luxury apartment complex offers modern urban living combined with quiet neighborhood charm. Unit 4B is a spacious 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment featuring premium hardwood floors, large floor-to-ceiling windows with south-facing views, and an open-concept kitchen equipped with energy-efficient stainless steel appliances. The property includes central air conditioning, smart home thermostat integration, high-security deadbolt locks, and pre-wired high-speed fiber optic internet connections throughout every room.

2. FINANCIAL TERMS: RENT, LATE FEES, AND DEPOSIT STRUCTURE
The monthly rent for Unit 4B is set at $2,250, payable on or before the 1st day of each calendar month via the secure online portal. Payments made after the 5th day of the month will incur a mandatory late fee of $75. A security deposit of $2,250 is required upon signing this lease agreement, which is fully refundable at the end of the tenancy subject to a standard property inspection for damages. Additionally, a non-refundable one-time pet deposit of $300 is mandatory for tenants keeping approved domestic pets, alongside a monthly pet rent of $25 per animal.

3. UTILITIES, MAINTENANCE, AND EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS
Monthly rent includes standard trash removal, pest control, and community sewage services. The tenant is individually responsible for electricity, high-speed internet, and water utility bills based on monthly consumption meters. Routine maintenance requests can be submitted through the online tenant portal 24/7 and are typically resolved within 48 business hours. Emergency maintenance issues, such as major plumbing leaks, gas odors, or electrical failures, should be reported immediately to the 24/7 emergency hotline provided in your onboarding welcome packet.

4. AMENITIES, PARKING, AND COMMUNITY GUIDELINES
Residents have full, complimentary access to the community fitness center, heated outdoor swimming pool, and rooftop terrace garden from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily. Designated parking space #42 is assigned exclusively to Unit 4B. Smoking is strictly prohibited inside all indoor common areas, hallways, and individual residential units. Quiet hours are strictly enforced between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM every night to ensure a peaceful community environment for all neighboring residents.
