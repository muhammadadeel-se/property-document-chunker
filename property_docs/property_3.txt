PROPERTY LEASE AGREEMENT & WELCOME PACKET - PINEWOOD ESTATES

1. PROPERTY DESCRIPTION AND INDOOR FEATURES
Pinewood Estates Unit 8C is an expansive 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom suburban townhome located at 305 Pine Valley Road. Featuring a private fenced backyard, attached two-car garage, wood-burning fireplace in the main living room, and a generous walk-in master bedroom closet, this property provides exceptional comfort, storage space, and privacy for families. High-end carpeting, vaulted ceilings, and energy-double-pane windows come standard throughout the entire residence.

2. FINANCIAL POLICIES: RENT, DEPOSIT, AND PROCESSING FEES
The monthly rent for this property is $3,100, due on the first of each month via the automated tenant payment portal. A security deposit of $3,500 is required prior to taking possession of the keys. A refundable key and garage remote deposit of $150 is also collected at lease signing. Returned checks, disputed charges, or failed ACH transactions will incur a $40 bank processing fee, and late rent payments attract a flat fee of $100 after the fifth of the month.

3. UTILITIES, LAWN CARE, AND PROPERTY UPKEEP
The tenant is directly responsible for all household utilities, including electricity, natural gas, municipal water, high-speed internet, and weekly curbside trash collection. Routine front yard lawn care and community landscaping maintenance are managed and paid for by the landlord, while the tenant is strictly responsible for maintaining the private backyard grass area and patio in a clean, neat, and orderly condition year-round.

4. COMMUNITY AMENITIES AND GUIDELINES
Pinewood Estates features a private neighborhood children's playground, professional tennis courts, and a community clubhouse available for private party reservations by residents. Homeowners association (HOA) rules apply to all residents; printed copies of the full HOA bylaws and community covenants are provided in the welcome folder. Unregistered commercial vehicle storage and major automotive repairs are strictly prohibited in the driveway or common parking areas.
